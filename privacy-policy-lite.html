Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Lineva Lite handles information when you use the application.

Edition: Lineva Lite Last Updated: May 16, 2026 Contact: husain.abdurrohim@gmail.com

This page is intended to be used as the public privacy policy URL for the Lite edition of Lineva.

1. Overview

Lineva Lite is a free vector design application for mobile and desktop devices. This policy describes how information related to your use of the application is processed and managed.

2. Information We Handle

Lineva Lite primarily handles project content that you create, edit, import, export, or back up where available in your app edition within the application. This may include vector shapes, layers, canvas settings, metadata, and files that you choose to import into the application.

3. Local Storage and Device Data

Project data is primarily stored locally on your device or within the local application environment. Backup files that you export are saved to locations selected or permitted by your device and operating system.

4. Backup and Restore

Backup and restore behavior depends on your app edition. When these features are available and used, the application processes your project files to generate exportable backup content or to import existing backup content that you provide. These operations are initiated by you and are intended to support local data portability and recovery.

5. Cloud Services

Lineva Lite does not provide built-in cloud synchronization for project data. Your projects and backups are not intended to be automatically synced to a remote service by the application itself unless such behavior is introduced in a future version and expressly disclosed.

6. How Information Is Used

Information handled by the application is used to operate core features, including project creation, editing, previewing, importing, exporting, backing up, and restoring. The information is also used to preserve document structure and improve continuity of your work within the application.

7. User Responsibility

You are responsible for the content you create, import, export, or store through the application. You are also responsible for maintaining your own backup practices and for protecting access to devices or storage locations that contain your project files.

8. Data Security

Reasonable care is expected in the design and operation of the application. However, no method of digital storage or file transfer can be guaranteed to be completely secure or error-free. You should keep independent backups of important work.

9. Third-Party Platform Behavior

Your device, operating system, browser, or app distribution platform may apply separate data handling rules, permissions, or storage behavior. Those third-party terms and privacy policies may also affect how files are stored, accessed, or shared outside the application itself.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any revised version becomes effective on the stated last updated date shown on this page.
